## Approved claims

- The software is a bounded research simulation for proposal support.
- The architecture separates essential local protection from advisory cascade forecasting.
- The simulation includes a first-class `unassured` state when physical assumptions fail.
- The result graphics and metrics are proposal-support artifacts, not validation evidence.
- The 30-year LUNAR SENTINEL vision remains the end state, but the current build supports only a TRL <= 2 feasibility discussion.

## Prohibited claims

- Do not claim 30-year safety.
- Do not claim NASA readiness, NASA endorsement, or flight validation.
- Do not claim certification savings in dollars or months.
- Do not claim no recertification.
- Do not claim superiority over deployed NASA systems.
- Do not claim arbitrary node-loss or blackout survivability.
- Do not claim TRL 6.

## Required language
