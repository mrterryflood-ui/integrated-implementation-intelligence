# Owner directive — Dartmouth is one stakeholder, not the destination
Date: 2026-11-08. Binding clarification to the approved DIS–RPLICE Health mission, sprint plan and Dartmouth read-ahead.

## Governing correction
The Dartmouth meeting is a time-bound opportunity for scientific review, collaboration and replication, **not the ultimate customer, the program end state, or the boundary of the research**. Do not redefine research scope, priority or implementation strategy around one institutional stakeholder. The mission is to develop and validate an integrated, prevention-oriented, cross-condition intelligence and implementation method that improves real decisions and ultimately patient and population outcomes.

## Beneficiary and stakeholder ecosystem
- **Ultimate beneficiaries:** patients, families and communities experiencing delayed recognition, avoidable disease burden, inequitable care or preventable harm.
- **Operational users:** clinicians, care teams, community health workers, public health analysts, implementation teams, health-system leaders and authorized research investigators.
- **Decision and resource stakeholders:** health systems, community organizations, payers, public agencies, registries, policy leaders, research sponsors and technology/data stewards.
- **Scientific partners:** Dartmouth is one potential partner alongside VA, UTHealth, registry programs and other qualified collaborators. No partner role or data access is presumed.
- **Governance:** user/owner retains mission authority and external commitment approval; patient/community and clinical perspectives must inform implementation and measurement.

## Parallel workstreams — not a linear Dartmouth-only funnel
A. **Research:** source provenance, comparability, historical first-signals, population-specific risk, counterfactuals, multi-outcome measurement, cross-condition falsification.
B. **Independent assurance:** adversarial source replication, conflicting evidence, test execution, claim adjudication and governance audit.
C. **Engineering:** research pipeline reproducibility, persistent evidence ledger, security, versioning, reliable integrations and end-to-end tests; no unauthorized auth or production changes.
D. **Implementation:** workflow ownership, patient experience, capacity, feasibility, prospective evaluation and sustainment.
E. **Scale-out:** CRC first calibration; autoimmune/endometriosis next named domains; lung and other conditions as justified methodological comparisons, without transferring clinical thresholds.
F. **Stakeholder and funding:** prepare Dartmouth briefing and independent scientific asks while mapping additional partners, funding and implementation customers separately; no outbound messages or submissions without approval.

## Definition of progress
Do not count Dartmouth slide production as completion of clinical research, implementation, deployment or evidence of public benefit. Track separate acceptance gates and receipts for research validity, intervention feasibility, engineering verification, independent challenge, cross-condition transfer, clinical outcomes and sustainability. Preserve the owner-adjudicated multidimensional outcome framework; never force either/or endpoints.
