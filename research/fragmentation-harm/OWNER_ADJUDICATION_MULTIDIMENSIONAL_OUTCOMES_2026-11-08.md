# Owner adjudication — outcomes are complementary, not either/or
Date: 2026-11-08. Authority: direct owner correction. Supersedes any framing in the Dartmouth draft or independent-judge reconciliation that asks the owner to choose late-stage incidence *instead of* early:late improvement.

## Binding decision
The mission requires a **multidimensional, jointly interpreted outcome framework**, not an exclusive primary-versus-secondary choice that suppresses a scientifically relevant construct. The following must be assessed together, with population/period/denominator definitions and uncertainty:
- Earlier signals, diagnostic opportunities, and time from first knowable warning to appropriate action;
- Age-specific risk and evidence-derived starting points for screening where appropriate, distinct from symptomatic evaluation at any age;
- Early-stage and late-stage case counts and age-adjusted incidence rates;
- Early:late ratios and stage distribution, explicitly accounting for ascertainment, overdiagnosis, and stage migration;
- All-stage incidence, mortality, emergency diagnosis, survival where interpretable, function and quality of life;
- Patient experience, access, inequity, intervention capacity, false positives, overtreatment, costs and durability.

These are not interchangeable and cannot be reduced to a single number. Analyses may predefine primary statistical estimands for a particular experiment to control error rates and sample-size planning, but this **does not redefine the mission or remove other outcome domains**. Interpret conflicting movement as a research finding requiring causal investigation, not as permission to select a convenient metric.

## Application to Hinds/Harris
Late-stage incidence gap 6.0 per 100k, case-share gap 0.3 percentage points, all-stage incidence gap 8.4 per 100k. Their joint divergence is the point of calibration; a decomposition is descriptive, not causal. Preserve all constructs, explicitly label age-adjusted rate quotient versus case-based share.
