# DIS–RPLICE Health | Mission Charter and Cross-Agent Research Contract
**Authority:** Owner-directed mission and subsequent explicit corrections, November 8, 2026. The destination is fixed; methods and sequencing adapt with evidence. This revision applies the owner's decisions to the governing charter itself, rather than leaving them only in separate addenda. Previous versions remain in Git history.

## Mission — fixed destination
Develop and validate an integrated, prevention-oriented research-to-action system that measures from the earliest observable risk or warning signal, identifies who is missed and why, closes the loop to appropriate completed action, and tests whether earlier action reduces late discovery and avoidable harm. Establish where early diagnoses can outnumber late diagnoses, using condition-appropriate definitions and the full set of patient and population outcomes. CRC is the initial calibration, not the mission or the product boundary.

Primary prevention can precede symptoms. Examine biological and life-course risk, family history, environment and occupational exposures, social conditions, policy, incentives, institutions, clinical capacity, resources, technology, and lived experience. Do not assert that all healthcare starts counting at diagnosis; investigate where diagnosis-indexed measures conceal earlier knowable opportunities. Do not presume that early labeling or every earlier test produces benefit.

The ChainWeb hypothesis asks whether interconnected upstream measurement/decision failures contribute to multiple downstream effects, and whether correcting a verified mechanism improves direct, connected and sustained outcomes. Causation, preventability, spillover and transfer remain hypotheses until supported by the relevant evaluation.

## Fixed platform hierarchy — no substitution
- **DIS:** core multidisciplinary intelligence, whole-system understanding, orchestration, mission alignment, decision/implementation governance and learning.
- **RPLICE:** core research-to-practice, evidence/methods, implementation-analysis and evaluation engine. DIS and RPLICE are interdependent drivers, enablers and the principal build/research focus.
- **HerHealth:** owner-described **93-condition** supporting catalog, collaborative starting point and potential consumer of reviewed findings. It is not the core mission.
- **Mental-health platform:** owner-described **60-condition** supporting catalog and collaborator. This is **mental health, not men's health**. Do not silently replace one with the other. Other platforms may contribute where mission-relevant and authorized.
- Supporting platforms provide candidate signals, contextual evidence, service knowledge and tertiary corroboration/support. They can help select investigations and implement verified findings. Repetition of one original study across platforms is not independent corroboration. Trace primary evidence, original lineages, dates and claim limits before promotion.
- Catalog counts do not establish that every entry is a distinct condition, has verified disproportionate burden, or is clinically validated. Preserve the owner's scope counts separately from source-code tags, seed counts, aliases, content topics and live database records; reconcile differences without silently redefining scope.
- Patients, families and communities are ultimate beneficiaries. Dartmouth is one potential stakeholder; Monday's partnership accelerator is a separate workstream, not this platform's agenda, delivery gate or scientific acceptance.

## Six design obligations
M1. **Consistent measurement from first signals:** preserve patient-experienced onset, first recorded signal, first clinician-available signal, decision, action and outcome separately. Observation, publication, revision and retrieval times are also distinct. Do not invent a time zero where the source does not support one.
M2. **Defensible population-specific starting points:** evaluate risk-based timing rather than making age 45 a software gate. Symptomatic diagnostic evaluation, hereditary surveillance, asymptomatic screening and primary prevention are different decisions. Derive and validate starting points, absolute risks, calibration, net benefit and harms; a mathematical transformation alone is not a screening recommendation.
M3. **Closed-loop appropriate action:** distinguish known signal, recognition, feasible decision, authorized action, completed follow-through and outcomes. Preserve unknown, declined, deferred and outside-system care. An alert or referral is not completed care.
M4. **Explain differences behind similar headlines:** reconcile instruments, constructs, denominators, populations, periods, ascertainment and source lineage before interpreting differences. Preserve polypectomy/precursor removal, underlying incidence, population composition, access/capacity, stage migration and missingness as rival explanations where relevant.
M5. **Continuous learning:** source and version changes, failed tests, contradictory findings, patient/frontline experience, implementation fidelity, resource use and independent review feed the next cycle. Preserve baseline and historical failures; no silent evidence replacement.
M6. **Proof of meaningful improvement:** jointly interpret earlier/later diagnosis, incidence, mortality/emergency presentation where relevant, symptoms/function, experience, equity, false positives, overdiagnosis, workload, costs and sustainability. These are complementary, not either/or. Individual experiments may prespecify statistical estimands without discarding other consequential outcomes.

## Invariants and scaling discipline
1. Every work item names the mission obligation and a concrete decision or uncertainty it advances. No agent substitutes a disease, convenient screening workflow, partner, funding task or interface feature for the mission.
2. Deep research includes primary studies, source definitions, contrary evidence, qualitative/experiential inputs and reproducible analysis. Distinguish observation, association, prediction, decision quality, causal effect, transportability and prevented harm.
3. Apply DIS MAP–GAP: ORIENT -> AUDIT -> MAP -> GAP -> ALIGN -> ACT -> TRACK -> ADAPT -> SHARE -> RE-MAP. Compare Designed, Operational and Experienced realities. Complement existing services instead of rebuilding them without need.
4. **Scale up** scientific depth and reliable capacity within a pathway: longitudinal sources, subgroup validity, source reconciliation, decision quality, operational integration, testing and recovery. More sources or passing tests alone are not more validity.
5. **Scale out** the validated method across conditions, populations, settings and implementation partners. Prepare autoimmune disease and endometriosis in parallel with CRC; mental-health content supplies relevant cross-system context and candidate mechanisms. Do not require all CRC clinical questions to be solved before lawful exploratory work elsewhere. Do not transfer clinical thresholds or effect sizes without evidence.
6. **Scale through implementation and sustainment:** specify accountable owners, people/time/cost, clinical authority, feasibility, fidelity, uptake, equity, maintenance, reassessment and rollback. A connected API is not proof of changed care or benefit.
7. One repeatable program: Scope -> Validate -> Integrate -> Demonstrate -> Measure -> Expand, with feedback to any earlier phase. Each condition/setting has its own status. These phases are not six separate products.
8. Four user-visible steps: describe the problem; see evidence and gaps; receive a justified plan; execute authorized work and learn. Broad intake is allowed; validated claims stay condition-specific. Preserve visible panels for what is known, assumed and unknown.
9. Independent judge review is separate from author self-checks. Record exact source/code versions, accepted claims, failed claims and dissent. Agent 1 builds science; Agent 2 exercises the actual lifecycle; the judge independently challenges; the owner adjudicates authority and commitments. Assigned lanes do not imply agents were launched.
10. Counterfactuals use information available at decision time, eligible comparison populations, feasible alternatives, confounding/selection checks, negative controls and harms. Compare integrated intelligence with competent equal-time current practice; justified nonchange is a possible correct result.
11. Evidence storage requires original bytes, manifests, lawful access, source rights, versioned outputs and a witnessed restore. Private Drive preservation is not institutional immutable retention or a public DOI. Maintain distinct engineering, source, independent-review and clinical-effect receipts.
12. No invented records, completion, partners, eligibility or validation. No clinical use, patient-level access, public deposition, external messaging, spending or deployment without applicable authorization. Do not interfere with parallel authentication work. Explicit HIL method selection is not a blanket methodological HOLD.

## Scientific completion gates
A. Source and measurement comparability reproduced and adjudicated.
B. Upstream signals and action windows evaluated with uncertainty, missed cases and negative evidence.
C. Competing mechanisms tested, including evidence loss, selection, clinical capacity and experienced barriers.
D. Counterfactual/decision-quality design evaluated with relevant bias and harm checks.
E. Appropriate action/process change demonstrated and patient outcomes evaluated separately.
F. Connected effects, distribution, sustainment and transfer independently evaluated.
G. Methods, provenance, durable records, independent challenge and decisions reproducible.

Software completion, literature synthesis and clinical effectiveness are distinct. Do not mark the entire system complete because one gate or condition passes. Do not suppress useful public research merely because a later patient-data gate is not yet accessible.
