# DIS–RPLICE Health | Mission Charter and Cross-Agent Research Contract
**Authority:** User-directed governing mission, November 8, 2026. Applies to all agents and future research work. **Status:** Mission invariant; changes require explicit owner approval.

## Mission — do not redefine
Develop, rigorously test, and validate an integrated, prevention-oriented healthcare research and implementation intelligence system that can combine heterogeneous evidence across people, institutions, technology, policy, resources, geography, time, and lived experience; detect hidden risk and missed opportunities *before disease progression*; diagnose failures in measurement and decisions; test counterfactual interventions; and establish whether improvements produce durable, equitable, transferable patient and system benefits.

The scientific question is whether apparently sound isolated metrics and instruments conceal materially different underlying realities, leading to misleading comparisons, missed preventive opportunities, or ineffective interventions. The ChainWeb hypothesis asks whether connected upstream failures produce multiple downstream effects and whether addressing a verified mechanism improves outcomes beyond one pathway. **Neither causal harm nor spillover is presumed.**

## Invariants
1. **Mission first:** No agent may silently substitute a narrower disease, clinical workflow, model, funding task, or product feature for the overarching research mission.
2. **Pilots are calibration, not scope:** Hinds County MS versus Harris County TX colorectal cancer is one calibration case; screening/referral completion is a subset, not the main goal. Expand to earlier signs, longitudinal risk, social/environmental context, other diseases and geographies only to test defined questions.
3. **Deep, precise research:** Independently examine authoritative sources, current peer-reviewed primary evidence, contrary findings, raw definitions, denominators, populations, instruments, historical availability, confidence intervals, uncertainty, and biases. Never conflate association, prediction, measurement discrepancy, causation, implementation success, or prevented harm.
4. **Whole-system DIS:** Compare Designed, Operational and Experienced realities. Apply MAP–GAP: ORIENT -> AUDIT -> MAP -> GAP -> ALIGN -> ACT -> TRACK -> ADAPT -> SHARE -> RE-MAP. Investigate people, incentives, policy, capacity, information, and lived experience, not technology alone.
5. **Counterfactual rigor:** Define decision time and only information available then; test plausible alternative actions, exchangeability, confounding, selection, missingness, negative controls, adverse effects and falsification. Distinguish pre-screening prevention, symptomatic diagnosis, familial-risk surveillance, routine screening and follow-up.
6. **Traceable end-to-end proof:** Source -> provenance -> construct -> extraction -> harmonization -> population/denominator -> time -> analysis -> interpretation -> actionable decision -> outcome -> sustainment. For initial calibration also trace actual published clinical pathways, identifying missing local patient-level records explicitly.
7. **Cross-disease transfer is conditional:** Transfer the *scientific method* across disease domains, not unvalidated clinical thresholds or assumed causal effects. Investigate women’s health, lung cancer and other systems where evidence warrants.
8. **No goalpost shifting:** New findings are classified as supporting, contradicting, refining or opening a subquestion. They do not replace the mission or acceptance criteria without user authorization. Keep a change log and rationale.
9. **Independent agent verification:** A second agent may audit claims, challenge assumptions, reproduce calculations, inspect CI and independently review literature. Record disagreements, adjudication and disposition; do not overwrite dissent or declare consensus without evidence.
10. **Execution fidelity:** Prioritize original empirical questions over synthetic demonstrations, placeholder features, repetitive status reports or unnecessary PRs. Deliver reproducible artifacts, exact GitHub receipts, tests, open gaps, accountable owners and next scientific action.
11. **Boundaries:** Do not invent data or citations; do not claim access to patient records, clinical impact, production functionality or completed research without proof. Require appropriate authorization, privacy/IRB governance and data-use arrangements for patient-level research. No external communication, expenditure or deployment without owner authorization. Do not interfere with parallel login remediation.

## Scientific completion gates
A. Reproduced and adjudicated source comparability across selected populations and periods.
B. Validated upstream signals and earliest actionable windows, including negative evidence.
C. Tested explanations for data loss, selection bias, care capacity and missed opportunities.
D. Defined and evaluated appropriate counterfactuals, with uncertainty and competing harms.
E. Demonstrated whether interventions change decisions/processes and, separately, patient outcomes.
F. Independently assessed ChainWeb cross-system effects and transferability.
G. Reproducible methods, provenance, peer/agent challenge, decision ledger, governance, sustainment and evidence-qualified conclusions.

**Current scientific state:** Published evidence establishes some measurement/representation discrepancies and process interventions; Hinds–Harris patient-level causal prevention effect and cross-disease ChainWeb benefits remain unproven. Do not close gates without receipts.

## Required handoff format for every agent
- Mission alignment: which invariant and research question this work advances.
- Evidence: primary source, source date, extract, construct, denominator, period, reproducible calculation.
- Decision: verified / inferred / hypothesized / contradicted / unknown.
- Counterfactual and competing explanations; equity and adverse effects.
- Executed work: exact files, branch, commit, CI, reviews and validation receipts.
- Open gaps: root cause, owner, access dependencies, next experiment, closure criteria.
- Scope-change request: explicit owner approval before changing mission or primary acceptance gates.
