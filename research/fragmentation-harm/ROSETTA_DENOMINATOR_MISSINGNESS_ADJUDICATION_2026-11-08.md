# DIS–RPLICE Health | Rosetta Stone denominator and fragmentation adjudication protocol
**Date:** 2026-11-08. **Status:** binding research-methods candidate for independent adjudication; not a new workstream, patient dataset, completed harmonization or clinical authorization. **Existing owner register:** issue #81, W2/W4/W8, PR #85 science, PR #86 review, PR #87 lifecycle, RPLICE PR #118 comparator. **Judging role:** independent methodological challenge and evidence-bound acceptance, not author self-certification.

## Decision and nonnegotiable distinction
We do **not** clean dirty data by coercing it to agree. We preserve raw observations, source-defined meaning and missingness, produce versioned transformations, compare only like estimands and retain contradictions. If the source does not contain the needed denominator, create a **denominator specification and acquisition/derivation task**, not a fabricated denominator or synthetic patient record. A newly derived or proxy quantity must remain distinguishable from the original and from an observed count.

## Denominator identity: required for each measure
Every comparison candidate must have a versioned tuple:
`{source_id, release_id, retrieval_time, geography_id, geographic_boundary_vintage, condition_definition, case_definition, population_definition, numerator_definition, denominator_definition, observation_start, observation_end, decision_time_cutoff, stage_definition, unknown_stage_rule, suppression_rule, age_partition, standard_population, sex_scope, race_ethnicity_scope, inclusion_exclusion, units, measure_type, estimator, rounding, uncertainty_method, data_quality_flags, transformation_id, original_sha256, reviewer_disposition}`.
For case shares: distinguish late-stage numerator from **all staged**, **all incident**, and **known-stage-only** denominator candidates; do not select without source-specific release metadata. For incidence: denominator is eligible resident person-time/annual population with age standardization, **not** incident cancer cases. For prospective pathway: denominator is **all eligible first-signal episodes at decision time**, including those not eventually diagnosed, with entry, censoring, loss, competing outcomes and missing follow-up defined. Maintain unit-of-analysis and clustering (people/episodes/clinics/counties) separately.

## Missingness and attribution ontology (no silent zero-fill)
Use **OBSERVED_PRESENT**, **OBSERVED_ZERO**, **NOT_COLLECTED**, **NOT_IN_THIS_SOURCE**, **PERFORMED_ELSEWHERE_UNVERIFIED**, **PERFORMED_ELSEWHERE_VERIFIED**, **NOT_PERFORMED_CONFIRMED**, **DECLINED**, **DEFERRED**, **IN_PROGRESS**, **LOST_TO_FOLLOWUP**, **SUPPRESSED**, **NOT_APPLICABLE**, **INVALID**, **SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE**, **LINKAGE_FAILED**, **UNKNOWN**. Each status carries `source, ascertainment_method, timestamp, adjudicator, confidence_basis, next_verification_action`. `UNKNOWN` must not silently become `NOT_PERFORMED_CONFIRMED`; `SUPPRESSED` must not become zero. Missingness can be informative; show it by geography, subpopulation, institution, and observation time, with eligible denominator and sensitivity to selection. Where comparable proxy data are used, require same estimand and validity evidence; label proxies separately and preserve bias bounds.

## Retrospective and prospective clocks
**Retrospective:** freeze historical decision date, eligible cohort at that date, source availability then, source vintage, signal and action timeline, follow-up window, outcomes and censoring. Separate modern reconstructed truth from then-knowable information. Compare alternative decisions using only contemporaneous inputs, retain negative/undiagnosed controls, and distinguish ecological correlations from individual counterfactuals.
**Prospective (future authorized evaluation):** preregister eligibility and entry time, standard of care and alternatives, source-refresh cadence, patient and clinician input, intervention exposure, actual actions, completion, experience, adverse effects, resource/capacity, missing follow-up, subgroup heterogeneity and sustainment. Do not open a clinical data stream, change guidance, contact sites or enroll patients without specific authority.

## Harmonization decision algorithm
1. Preserve exact original bytes, license/access class, codebook and hashes.
2. Identify intended estimand and unit; record all denominator fields.
3. Classify structural/semantic/temporal/geographic differences and missingness before merging.
4. If definitions match, reproduce original statistic and uncertainty. If bridgeable, publish a versioned crosswalk and loss/bias sensitivity; if not bridgeable, retain separate estimates and explanatory comparability flags.
5. Independently challenge with alternative coding, age bands, time windows, selection thresholds, negative controls, and state-specific heterogeneity.
6. Record resulting change to actual decision, feasible response, cost, burden, equity and unintended effects. Distinguish improved ascertainment, prediction, decision, action, health and sustainment.
7. Handoff to existing reconciler with **artifact SHA, expected integration target, owner ACK, applied commit, tests, reviewer disposition, live readback and closure criterion**. No ACK means DELIVERED_UNCONFIRMED, not completed.
8. Reopen dependent claims when source versions, definitions, capacity, policies or evidence change; maintain previous versions.

Treat this as methods/implementation research with preregistered comparator and independent reviewers; avoid novelty and clinical-effect claims before verification.
