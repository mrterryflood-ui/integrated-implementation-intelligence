## What the numbers establish
- Same H80 program, different reported outcomes: 2024 CRC screening 33.93% TX, 37.32% MS, 38.37% CO, 48.13% NC, 48.05% NY, 42.55% CA; descriptive range14.20pp. Not risk-adjusted or attributable to a named institution/payer. NC Medicaid-primary share28.82% versus CA72.03% does not track their screening48.13% versus42.55% monotonically; this does NOT establish a Medicaid treatment effect.
- Known-income poverty percentages89.43%-93.92% hide unknown income26.20%-38.38%. Texas's92.16% conditional low-income figure has worst-case all-reported-patient bounds60.83%-94.83%. These are identification bounds, not confidence intervals or imputations.
- UDS Medicare-Medicaid duals are a subset of Medicare, not an extra primary category. Mississippi Medicaid/CHIP-primary26.53% becomes32.06% when the separately reported dual subset is included. This is not a census of all Medicaid enrollment. Primary insurance is at last visit; it is not the payer on every claim.
- The official diabetes adverse numerator includes HbA1c>9%, missing values and tests not performed. Preserve the official measure and distinguish its components for remediation; do not relabel all adverse observations as biologically uncontrolled diabetes.
- Chan/Card/Taylor AER2023,401,319 ambulance rides2001-2014 among VA+Medicare-eligible65+ veterans: published IV mortality effect-4.5pp(95%CI-8.0,-1.1), spending-$2,598(~21.18% of$12,265 mean). OLS spending+$840. Different estimands/selection assumptions can reverse direction; the difference is not a quantified bias estimate. No row-level model rerun or current universal VA superiority.
- Gaye2021 NYC safety-net eConsult: scheduling+15.8pp, adjusted wait-8.2days, but adjusted90-day visit completion-0.8pp(P=.07); no established completion improvement.3,074/23,529 requests resolved without a visit(13.06%) remain a distinct valid endpoint. Source wait baseline discrepancy60.1table vs61.0prose retained; no silently chosen crude effect.
- Asfaw2024 RCI:24/24 within-VA overall wait contrasts have95%CI containing0; not equivalence or24 independent trials. The clock ends on completed OR canceled, not verified completed care. Community GI centralized contrast-15.2days(-26.4,-4.0) prevents an indiscriminate failure claim; hybrid community results have failed parallel-trend qualification.
- Emerson/Hughes2023 single FQHC look-alike: Medicaid initiation7.4%, private5.6%, self-pay3.8%; conditional completion33.0%,35.6%,32.1%, respectively. No statistically established completion difference; not equivalence. Income and costs not measured; selection into referral remains important.
