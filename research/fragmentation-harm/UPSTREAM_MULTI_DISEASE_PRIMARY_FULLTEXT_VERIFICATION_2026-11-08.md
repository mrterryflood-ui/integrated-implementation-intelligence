## Evidence-to-decision chain across conditions
The primary phenomenon is **misleading or delayed action from incomplete, differently measured, or differently interpreted information across systems**, beginning *before* conventional screening. The same audit method can apply to multiple diseases, but disease-specific thresholds, intervention effects and ethical tradeoffs are NOT transferable by assertion.

### A. Earlier-than-screening longitudinal risk: measured performance vs actual action
**Primary full text:** Virdee PS et al. 2026, BMC Cancer, published 2026-05-14, DOI https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-026-16179-9.
**Original data:** CPRD AURUM English primary-care EHR 2000–2018; adults >=40 with hemoglobin, MCV, platelet data and eligible registry linkage. Main validation N=2,956,977 men + 3,561,349 women = **6,518,326**. Models derived in CPRD GOLD; GP systems differed (EMIS vs Vision), with migrating practices excluded.
**Exact construct:** age and historical FBC trends to predict diagnosis about two years later. Main *hybrid retrospective* sample chose index test using known diagnosis/exit date and excluded blood tests within two years before that date; observed diagnoses concentrated at two years (+/-3 months) after selected index. **This selection cannot be directly treated as a prospectively implemented live-time intervention.** In a no-two-year-washout sensitivity cohort including tests closer to diagnosis, AUC increased from 0.73 to 0.84 in men and from 0.74 to 0.91 in women. Improved short-horizon detection does NOT establish 2+ years earlier predictive utility.
**Cross-source ascertainment:** NCRAS-alone CRC outcomes 12,578 male + 11,939 female = 24,517. Expanded NCRAS+CPRD+HES+ONS outcomes 13,423 + 12,919 = **26,342**, +845 men and +980 women = **1,825 additional identified outcomes (7.44% more than NCRAS-only tally)** under alternate source definition. This is *ascertainment dependence*, not proof 1,825 missed clinical cancers or registry error. The outcome definition, linkage and overlap differ.
**Selection/utility:** highest one-percent predicted-risk group captured just 3.56% of male and 5.03% of female cancers (sensitivity); published PPV range <2% even at assessed thresholds. Overall age-adjusted trend discrimination was roughly comparable to age-adjusted binary FBC abnormality, despite evidence that trends can occur before abnormal thresholds. Models under-predicted some age, deprivation and ethnic strata; published net-benefit curves concern model utility against treat-all/none, not proven care or mortality. No accepted clinical CRC-specific FBC risk threshold.
**Critical counterfactual:** Would reporting these trends *in real time to an accountable decision-maker with feasible, acceptable actions* improve decisions relative to contemporary symptom, history, and conventional lab assessment? This cannot be answered by AUC alone. Falsify with age+abnormality comparator, prospective temporal cutoffs, subgroup calibration, false positive/referral burden, and patient outcomes.

### B. Intervention trial: higher crude follow-up is not a validated causal effect
**Primary full text:** Chima S et al., *Br J Gen Pract* 2024/2025 DOI https://doi.org/10.3399/BJGP.2024.0491; full text https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12010534/.
**Original study:** Pragmatic 40-practice cluster randomized active-control trial in Australia; 7,555 patients flagged for iron deficiency, thrombocytosis or elevated PSA, not a CRC-only cohort. Intervention FHT CDS/QI module + practice education/support. At 12 months: 2,820/3,709=76.03% vs 2,693/3,846=70.02% follow-up; raw difference **6.01 percentage points**. Published cluster-adjusted primary effect **2.6 pp (95% CI -2.8 to +7.9), p=0.332**, *not statistically established*. Prespecified additional sensitivity adjustment reports 5.3 pp (95% CI 0.1 to 10.4) but OR 1.12 (95% CI 0.87 to 1.45), p=0.388; analytic scales/specifications must be reconciled rather than promoting one positive interval. Cancer diagnosis and time-to-investigation outcomes not significantly improved. EMR ascertainment missed some free-text symptom and out-of-field referral content; trial took place during COVID disruptions.
**New full-text reconciliation flag:** Table 4 reports pooled follow-up count **5,507**, yet intervention 2,820 + control 2,693 = **5,513** (difference six); denominators 3,709+3,846=7,555 reconcile. This is an unresolved within-table count inconsistency. It neither negates the cluster-adjusted trial estimate nor authorizes silently replacing a published count. Requires author/source-data adjudication; no external contact made.
**Falsification:** A risk-alert product, even when a real RCT is run, may not produce clinically meaningful follow-through. Workflow ownership, human behavior, workload, baseline quality and care capacity are independent components of the ChainWeb mechanism.

### C. Women's health: literal instrument standards and time-anchor change observed gaps
**Primary full text:** Atkins S et al., *O&G Open* 2025 DOI https://doi.org/10.1097/og9.0000000000000054; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12456509/.
**Original study:** Single-institution retrospective cohort of **3,369** people with postmenopausal bleeding and first qualifying TVUS, 2013–2022. Reconstructed index signal and endometrial biopsy timing. Using a `within 3 months before OR after TVUS` definition, 307/1,671 **18.37%** with thickened endometrium and 128/389 **32.90%** with inadequately visualized endometrium lacked prompt histologic sampling. Requiring sampling *after* the index ultrasound and excluding prior samplings changes the denominator to 1,317 and 290 respectively, yielding **23.31%** and **44.14%** without prompt sampling. These are *two different eligible populations/time constructs*; the difference cannot be interpreted as deterioration over time.
**Direct competing-standards mechanism:** The full-text chart audit identified 21 incorrect radiographic interpretations; 19 by radiologists, and **17 of those 19** attributed to a mismatch between a 5-mm Society of Radiologists in Ultrasound threshold and a 4-mm ACOG threshold during the study period. This is observed evidence of discordant instrument interpretation/standards in a clinical pathway, not a proof that changing the cutoff prevents death. Chart adjudication identified clinician-related reasons for 46.1% of thickened and 58.4% of inadequate-visualization *delayed/absent follow-up* with restricted adjudicated subsets (217 and 106), not of all 3,369 patients.
**Critical counterfactual:** At the historical decision, which guideline was applicable, what did the clinician actually see, and would a harmonized, context-aware recommendation have produced an appropriate biopsy while controlling procedure harms? Cross-sectional observed differences alone do not estimate this effect.

### D. Lung health: transfer the investigation, not the effect size
**Primary published abstract only:** Singh H et al., *Respir Med* 2022 DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rmed.2022.106737. Prospective tracking program, nonrandomized comparison of lung nodule follow-up: 408/523 (78.01%) tracked vs 57/109 (52.29%) not tracked; observed RR **1.49**. Different probabilities of being assigned/tracked, nodule characteristics, severity and follow-up access can confound results. No claim of a randomized tracking effect or cancer/mortality reduction.

### E. Emerging extraction method (not independent clinical proof)
Hooman et al. VERGE (2026 preprint), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04366. Structured encounter fields can miss symptom timing/context and family history described in clinical notes; evaluated extraction precision on clinician-labeled note-finding pairs, not clinical diagnosis or intervention outcomes. Useful candidate for a separate documentation-comparability benchmark, NOT a patient-level prevention effect.

## Scientific triangulation and decision
- **VERIFIED:** alternative cancer ascertainment definition changes outcome tally by 1,825 in the BLOODTRACC study; study's main index depends on known future diagnosis/exit; no early clinical benefit measured.
- **VERIFIED:** a real multi-cancer CDS RCT did not establish a significant primary benefit; its aggregate table has six-record internal arithmetic discrepancy requiring adjudication.
- **VERIFIED:** in a women's health *observed* cohort, index timing and competing 4mm/5mm standards materially alter how appropriate follow-up is interpreted. This supplies strong independent mechanistic corroboration of the mission's *measurement and decision-context* concerns.
- **SUPPORTED, noncausal:** lung nodule tracking groups differ in observed follow-up.
- **UNTESTED:** integration-induced earlier diagnosis, reduction in disease progression, Hinds-Harris local actionability, causal ChainWeb spillovers, transportability and sustained equity benefit.
