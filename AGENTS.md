## Binding execution rules

- Preserve ISS LLC and TCAF as separate legal, financial, eligibility, contracting, evidence, and reporting entities.
- Diagnose before prescribing. Apply `Sense → Question → Understand → Diagnose → Decide → Act → Measure → Learn → Adapt`.
- Reuse existing platform contracts and complete the smallest connected vertical slice. Do not create parallel systems, orphan endpoints, decorative dashboards, or generic abstractions without a consuming journey.
- Treat email, aggregators, repository activity, builds, domains, health checks, acknowledgments, and relationship interest as signals—not proof of an opportunity, deployment, award, acceptance, outcome, or agreement.
- Verify decisive funding and procurement facts against the current authoritative notice: stable ID, mechanism, status, eligibility, entity, amendments, deadline/time zone, submission route, required forms, and receipt standard.
- A production deployment claim requires authoritative hosting evidence tying environment, deployment ID, status, timestamp, and exact source revision or immutable build manifest together.
- A review applies to an exact revision only when the provider explicitly associates the review with the full SHA. Otherwise final review is pending.
- Keep `Observed`, `Supported inference`, `User report`, `Hypothesis`, `Unknown`, and `Conflict` separate. Missing data is not zero. Preserve conflicts and fail closed.
- AI may research, compare, diagnose, draft, prepare reversible branches, run checks, and recommend. Dr. Flood retains consequence authority for production deployment, external submission, legal certification, contract or teaming commitment, expenditure, sensitive disclosure, and public/client publication.
- Keep customer and partner records tenant-isolated. Never reuse confidential evidence, pricing, proposal language, partner commitments, or eligibility conclusions across clients without authorization and renewed verification.
- Record every material unit as `Before → Changed → Why → Proof → Limits`, including owner, next action, review date, rollback, and evidence-completion rung.

## Engineering gates

Before proposing merge: inspect current head, active branches/PRs and deployment consequences; identify reuse/extend/new/not-touching/environment facts; run zero-error typecheck, relevant behavioral tests, affected-layer checks, security/privacy/tenancy checks, and independent exact-head review. Do not weaken a test to make a change pass. Merge is not deployment.

## Proposal and funding gates

Use the chain `Signal → Official source → Mechanism → Eligibility → Entity → Platform → Capability evidence → Capacity gaps → Capture decision → Proposal → Approval → Submission → Receipt → Decision → Delivery → Outcome → Learning`. Hard gates precede fit scores. Quarantine duplicates, expired notices, unsupported matches, conflicting amendments, unverified routes, and ineligible items.
