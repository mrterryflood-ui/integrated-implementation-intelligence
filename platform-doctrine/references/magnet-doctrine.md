# Magnet Doctrine

The magnet is the unifying systems doctrine behind the platforms: an
Internet-of-things philosophy in which events, patterns, domains, and stakeholders
are interconnected with zero gaps. It is what turns a collection of siloed public
systems into one common operating picture that tells a data story.

> "You are not building a hazard dashboard. You are building a magnet."

## What the magnet does

Public systems already observe, forecast, warn, plan, and record — but in silos,
each with its own clock, datum, and audience. The magnet pulls those systems onto
one picture at a point and does four things a person cannot do fast enough by hand:

1. **Finds the center of gravity** — the one node that decides the outcome.
2. **Lays out lines of operation** — tool → decision → action → owner, for each
   hazard clock.
3. **Maps every node to a line of effort** — life safety, food, energy, health,
   continuity, recovery, learning.
4. **Names every gap** — the dead gauge, the missing join, the role with no
   publisher, the handoff nobody timestamped, the causal claim with no comparison
   group.

Because the record is embedded, the same machine runs **backward as a time machine**
(retrospective/analog) and **forward as a synthesis engine** (forecast/decision).
The output is a **common operating picture per role** — a data story where every
sentence carries the source it came from, so a scientist can reproduce it and an
operator can act on it.

## The chain web and honest edges (chainweb)

The chain web is the interconnected cascade of impacts — "nothing happens in a
silo; even unrelated things have impact." The magnet renders it with a fixed rule:
**show everything, and say what kind of edge each link is.**

- `causal` — supported by a comparison group, shared time window, and
  difference-in-differences where the claim is quantitative.
- `correlated` / `needs control` — observed association, no defensible control yet.
- `not-causal` / `associated` — shown, but named as observation, not cause.

An unlabeled link is itself a gap, and the magnet names it. The magnet never
overstates causality; when a claim is causal it says so, and when it is not it says
that too.

## Simplicity is the floor

Easy to use and easy to understand is the foundation. The default face is plain
language, one decision at a time, no internal jargon. Deep interpretation and
research mode exist as a **toggle**, never as the default.

## The chain and human authority

The chain runs Sensed → Predicted → Happened → Learned, embodied as sensor → entity
→ story → person → receipt → outcome → next. The magnet may ingest, calculate,
compare, simulate, recommend, and draft — but it may not dispatch, close roads,
direct units, or issue public warnings. That stays human-gated.

## Relationship to the other doctrine

The magnet is the *systems* layer; Diagnostic Implementation Science is the
*method* layer (diagnose before prescribing, decisions are the unit of value,
adaptive fidelity). Integrated Implementation Intelligence is the *packaging* layer
that carries both, with provenance and local sovereignty. They compose; none
replaces another.

## No new sensors

The magnet does not build new sensors or hardware. It is integration, intelligence,
communication, and dynamic implementation over systems that already exist.
