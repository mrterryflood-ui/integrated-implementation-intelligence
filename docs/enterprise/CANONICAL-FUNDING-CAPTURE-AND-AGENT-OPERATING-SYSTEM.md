# Canonical Funding, Capture, Proposal, and Agent Operating System

**Owner:** Dr. Terry D. Flood  
**Entities:** Integrated Services & Solutions LLC (ISS LLC) and Thriving Communities for All Foundation (TCAF)  
**Effective:** October 21, 2026  
**Purpose:** Give Dr. Flood one simple, repeatable collaborative system that finds and understands opportunities, develops relationships, produces winning proposals, executes approved submissions, captures receipts, and learns—while preserving human authority and ISS/TCAF/customer separation.

## 1. The simple operating model

Every funding or contracting item follows one chain:

> **Signal → Official source → Mechanism → Eligibility → Entity → Platform → Capability evidence → Capacity gaps → Capture decision → Proposal package → Approval → Submission → Receipt → Decision → Delivery → Outcome → Learning**

The system may automate retrieval, classification, verification, deduplication, matching, drafting, compliance checks, calendars, reminders, and package assembly. Dr. Flood retains authority for legal certifications, final submissions, contracts, commitments, spending, production deployment, and public consequential claims.

### 2.3 Cross-entity capital stacks

ISS and TCAF may participate in the same community outcome only when the solicitation and law permit it, each entity's role is necessary, budgets are separate, conflicts are disclosed, procurement/allowability is satisfied, IP/data terms are written, and no cost or claim is counted twice. TCAF may lead eligible public-benefit delivery while ISS supplies technology or services under an authorized arm's-length agreement; ISS may lead R&D/commercial work while TCAF supplies eligible community implementation or evaluation. The default is separation, not combination.

## 3. Mechanism-first qualification

Before scoring “fit,” the system must answer:

1. What mechanism is this—contract, grant, cooperative agreement, BAA, SBIR, CSO, OT, consortium project, subcontract, challenge, CRADA, pilot, investment or debt?
2. What is the authoritative current notice and stable identifier?
3. Is it open, forecast, watch, partner-only, prior-award, closed, amended or conflicted?
4. Which legal entity can participate, and in what role—prime, sub, vendor, partner, applicant, subrecipient or collaborator?
5. What applicant, geography, size, registration, membership, certification, cost-share, clearance, past-performance and capacity gates apply?
6. What platform capability is proven at an exact revision, and what remains proposed?
7. What is the deadline, time zone, submission route, form set and receipt standard?
8. What customer/funder relationship exists, and what capture work is missing?
9. What is the expected value to the enterprise, probability basis, cost to pursue, delivery burden, IP/data risk and strategic value?
10. Who decides, and what is the next reversible action?

Hard gates are evaluated before weighted scoring. A high mission-alignment score cannot overcome ineligibility, a closed date, an unsupported claim, a missing submission route, or insufficient delivery capacity.

## 4. Opportunity states

| State | Meaning | Automation allowed |
|---|---|---|
| SIGNAL | Raw email/listing/referral/feed item | Ingest, dedupe, preserve source |
| QUARANTINED | Authority, status or eligibility not verified | Research and request missing evidence |
| VERIFIED | Official source, mechanism, status, eligibility rules, deadline and route confirmed | Create verified record and reminders |
| MATCHED | Entity/platform/capability/capacity evaluated | Prepare bid/no-bid decision card |
| WATCH | Real but not currently actable | Monitor official source and relationships |
| PARTNER | Better as teaming/subaward/collaboration route | Develop target partner and workshare |
| PURSUE | Human-authorized preparation | Build capture plan and package |
| APPROVAL READY | Complete and independently reviewed | Prompt Dr. Flood with exact decision/action |
| SUBMITTED | Approved external action completed | Capture immutable package reference and official receipt |
| DECIDED | Awarded, not selected, amended, withdrawn or canceled | Update pipeline and learning record |
| DELIVERY | Award/contract being implemented | Track obligations, outputs, invoices, outcomes and risk |
| CLOSED/QUARANTINED | Expired, duplicate, ineligible, unsupported or conflicted | Retain reason; do not recycle silently |

## 5. The collaborative proposal factory

### A. Capture before drafting

The capture agent builds a dossier 90–180 days ahead: buyer/funder mission; local problem and population; acquisition/funding mechanism; budget and calendar; incumbent/current solution; stakeholders and decision roles; competitors; partners; required registrations; likely evaluation factors; customer language; demonstrations; gaps; win probability basis; next relationship action.

### B. Bid/no-bid

GrantPathPro produces a decision card with: official notice, entity, mechanism, value, deadline, strategic fit, verified capability, capacity, eligibility, partner need, pursuit cost, delivery burden, risks, alternatives and recommendation. Dr. Flood authorizes the pursuit; this does not authorize submission.

### C. Build the package

The proposal architect creates one workspace containing the frozen solicitation/amendments, compliance matrix, evaluator map, annotated outline, evidence library, win themes, requirements-to-capability matrix, technical/program design, implementation plan, management/staffing, partner workshare, schedule, budget/pricing, risks, past performance, outcomes/evaluation, sustainment, forms and portal-field map.

### D. Review in strands

- **Strand A:** writes the compliant proposal from approved evidence.
- **Strand B:** independently verifies requirements, evidence, claims, evaluator clarity, price/budget, risk, privacy/security and submission mechanics.
- **Deterministic gates:** completeness, page/character limits, required forms, names/identifiers, dates, math, cross-references, filenames, signatures, attachments and portal fields.
- **Human gate:** Dr. Flood receives the exact package, unresolved risks and recommended decision.

### E. Submit once and reconcile

After approval, the execution agent uses the authoritative portal or route, handles only the authorized package/version, and returns the confirmation number, timestamp/time zone, submitted artifacts, portal status and any follow-up requirement. Email acknowledgment is not an award. A package without an official receipt remains unverified.

### F. Learn

Questions, evaluator comments, debriefs, selections, nonselections, amendments, performance, invoices and outcomes update the capability evidence, boilerplate, relationships, risk library, pricing, tests and future qualification rules.

## 7. How agents are trained on Dr. Flood, customers and stakeholders

Every agent receives a versioned mission packet, not a generic prompt:

1. Dr. Flood's intent, preferences, decision rights, communication style and $5M/$5M objectives.
2. ISS and TCAF legal/entity profiles kept separate.
3. Owned platform registry, canonical repositories, deployment identities, claim ceilings and open MAP gaps.
4. Customer/partner profile for the specific task—mission, geography, population, governance, capacity, relationship evidence and authorization.
5. Stakeholder map—buyer, user, beneficiary, funder, influencer, partner, evaluator, implementer, data steward and approver.
6. Applicable eleven-skill doctrine, product-specific rules and forbidden claims.
7. Authoritative source hierarchy and freshness window.
8. Required input/output contract, acceptance tests, deadline and handoff.
9. Approval boundaries, privacy, data minimization, IP and disclosure rules.
10. Before/Changed/Why/Proof/Limits reporting and a required non-obvious lesson.

Customer records are tenant/client isolated. An agent working for one client cannot reuse confidential details, eligibility conclusions, pricing, partner commitments or proposal text for another without permission and an evidence review. Cross-client learning is limited to generalized process improvements and public/authorized evidence.

## 8. Human handoff protocol

The system should interrupt Dr. Flood only when his judgment, authentication or authority changes the outcome. Every prompt uses this compact card:

- **Decision needed:** one sentence.
- **Why now:** deadline/consequence.
- **Entity and platform:** ISS or TCAF; exact product.
- **Recommendation:** one primary path.
- **Evidence:** authoritative source and relevant receipts.
- **Options/tradeoffs:** no more than three.
- **Risk/limits:** what remains unknown.
- **Your action:** approve/reject/edit, authenticate, sign, certify, pay, upload, attend or provide missing fact.
- **Time required:** estimated human minutes.
- **If no action:** exact consequence and next safe state.

Human-only operations include identity proofing, MFA/CAPTCHA, legal certification, typed signature, tax/banking representations, portal attestation, credit-card payment, contract acceptance, sensitive disclosure, production deployment approval and final submission approval.

## 9. Automation architecture

### Intake

- Gmail/thread monitoring for agency, portal, reviewer, partner, client and deadline signals.
- GitHub exact-revision, CI, review and security signals.
- Hosting-provider deployment records.
- Grants.gov, SAM.gov and Texas procurement feeds/exports where authorized.
- Official agency pages, NSTXL/consortium portals, funder portals and partner referrals.

### Processing

- Stable-ID deduplication.
- Entity/client separation.
- Mechanism and source classification.
- Official-source verification and amendment comparison.
- Eligibility/capability/capacity gating.
- Deadline and milestone generation.
- Relationship and partner lookup.
- Draft capture and proposal workspaces.
- Deterministic compliance, math, attachment and version checks.

### Execution controls

- `A0 READ`: autonomous retrieval, comparison, classification, reminders and drafts.
- `A1 REVERSIBLE`: authorized branches, tests, draft PRs, draft packages and internal task updates.
- `A2 CONSEQUENCE GATE`: submission, certification, send, contract, spend, merge when not preauthorized, production deployment, public/client publication or sensitive disclosure.
- Idempotency key binds the approval to one exact action and package/version.
- Decision receipt precedes action; provider/portal receipt follows action.

### Outputs

- Morning material-change brief.
- Weekly executive decision board.
- 90-day capture calendar and rolling 18-month forecast.
- Proposal readiness and receipt dashboard.
- Registration/certification gap register.
- Partner/relationship next-action register.
- Platform source/deployment/claim dashboard.
- ISS and TCAF capital scoreboard with no double counting.

## 10. Capital targets and pipeline math

Planning floors:

- **ISS LLC:** $5M booked/awarded/executed value, reported separately by contracts, R&D awards, pilots/licenses/services and private/debt capital.
- **TCAF:** $5M awarded/committed/executed value, reported separately by government, foundation/corporate, coalition/subaward and earned program revenue.
- **Qualified pipeline:** minimum 4× coverage, or $20M ISS plus $20M TCAF, excluding raw signals and client-request totals.
- **Client impact portfolio:** track the total value of client proposals supported by GrantPathPro separately; do not call it ISS/TCAF funding.

Quarterly portfolio allocation should use expected value, strategic learning, relationship value, capacity burden, time to cash, delivery risk and outcome potential. No single award or customer should become an unplanned dependency.

## 12. Current official-source findings that shape the plan

- SAM.gov is the official centralized source for federal contract opportunities and entity registration; prime federal awards require the appropriate registration.
- Grants.gov is the official federal assistance search/application entry point; grant and cooperative-agreement records stay separate from procurement.
- SBIR/STTR is phased R&D; Phase III uses non-SBIR funding and commercialization/acquisition pathways.
- DoD prototype OTs and CSOs are distinct flexible acquisition mechanisms with their own participation and transition conditions.
- GSA MAS is a pre-negotiated vehicle, but readiness, pricing, compliance and ongoing sales justify pursuing it—not prestige alone.
- Texas CMBL is the state bidder database; VetHUB is a separate Texas SDV certification path, and Texas agencies/primes use those records for bid/subcontract outreach.
- CRADAs are scoped collaborative R&D agreements and are not general-purpose funding.
- Texas SSBCI provides capital-access, guarantee and participation mechanisms through participating lenders; it is repayment capital, not an award.
- Challenge.gov was sunset March 30, 2026; federal challenges continue through USA.gov and agency sites, so monitoring must use the new official routes.

## 14. Completion and reporting

The operating system is successful when it reduces Dr. Flood's cognitive burden while increasing decision quality, on-time compliant submissions, relationship follow-through, source/deployment truth, receipt capture, funding conversion, delivery performance and measured outcomes. It is not successful merely because it generated more opportunities or more text.

Every material unit closes as:

> **Before → Changed → Why → Proof → Limits**

Every open limit has an owner, severity, next action and review date.
