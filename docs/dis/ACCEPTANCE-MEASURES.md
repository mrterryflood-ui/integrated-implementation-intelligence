# DIS acceptance measures and benchmark
All targets are PROPOSED thresholds for controlled evaluation, not achieved performance.

| Metric | Method | Proposed initial threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported material claim escape | Independent passage-level adjudication | 0 in high-stakes public reports |
| Citation exactness | Claim-to-source sample and contradiction audit | >=95% supported; all consequential claims adjudicated |
| Authorization-scope breach | Mutation/negative testing from simulated council approvals | 0 |
| Omitted critical dissent | Compare independent review records with synthesis | 0 material exclusions |
| Unwarranted causal/regulatory certainty | Red-team and golden examples | 0 |
| Missing-question identification | Human-constructed blind spot gold set | Recall >=80%, no false claims of completeness |
| Time-to-useful-answer | Instrumented task study | Baseline first; no universal target without pilot |
| End-user task completion | Observed scenario tasks and accessibility check | Baseline first, improve over comparator |
| Buyer/funder adoption | Real pilot and procurement discovery | Not measurable until engagement |
| Economic sustainability | Full costs, renewals, hosting, human review, support | Positive contribution after validated adoption assumptions |
| Transferability | Re-test across grant, healthcare, software, governance | Performance not materially worse without justified domain-specific adaptations |

Test protocol: identical source access and questions for DIS and comparator; independent second-runtime output; blinded scoring where available; publication of raw model/source traces (redacted for privacy); user consent for external actions. No fabricated benchmark performance.


## Longitudinal DIS self-assessment — proposed protocol (NOT ACHIEVED)
DIS must grade **its actual work** over time, not the fluency of its answers. Historical and contemporary sources, domain-specific instruments, stakeholder experience, implementation delivery and prospective predictions feed a learning loop without making the platform prescriptive.

| Dimension | Repeatable observation | What improvement would require | Checkpoints |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge quality | Independently adjudicated claim/support precision, outdated-source rate and correction latency | Demonstrated reduction in unsupported claims against a frozen blind benchmark | Baseline; 7, 30, 90 and 180 days |
| Research/engineering skill | Reproducible task success under instrumented end-to-end challenges, false-negative discovery and error recovery | Better out-of-sample performance without a new safety regression | Each release; 30, 90 and 180 days |
| Strategic calibration | Predeclared alternatives, forecasts, uncertainty and outcome predictions vs actual observations | Improved calibration and fewer confidently wrong decisions | Each retrospective/prospective review |
| Coordination/orchestration | Human-authorized handoff completion, correct attribution, latency, task abandonment and recoverability | Lower verified error/burden and higher real-user task completion | Per journey; weekly trend |
| Implementation science | Adoption, reach, acceptability, feasibility, fidelity, equity, cost, sustainment and unintended effects | Locally validated improvement relative to relevant baselines/comparators, with limitations | Pilot start; 30, 90, 180 days |
| Adaptation and continuity | Percentage of proposed changes with owner, evidence, approved test, observed follow-up and rollback | More effective, reversible interventions with intact core functions and privacy | Every accepted change; quarterly |

**Evidence rules:** Every observation records source, sample/denominator, cohort/setting, measurement date, instrument version, relevant counterfactual, human reviewer and limitations. A single opaque DIS score or Voltmeter is not accepted as scientific proof. The initial case-workflow counts are *documentation indicators*, not operational knowledge, real skill competence or verified social impact.

**Promotion gate:** A change to council selection, prompts, models, routing, policies, methods or shared strategic memory is a hypothesis until separately staged, independently reviewed, tested against historic and prospective holdouts, safety-checked, authorized and versioned. No unconsented reuse or cross-tenant learning. Document degradation and reverse changes that underperform.

**Feedback loop:** Observe → audit → diagnose error patterns → design competing adaptations → pilot on representative tasks → independently compare → approve/decline → update with provenance → monitor for drift. Reopen the decision when context or evidence changes; do not cement successful local strategies into universal truth.

Actual baselines, comparators and metric instrumentation are still required. Until then, the platform must label longitudinal self-improvement as an objective, not a measured outcome.
