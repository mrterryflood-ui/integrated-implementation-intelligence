# Early-action protocol: current-guidance and executable-rehearsal addendum
2026-11-07. Complements, and does not replace, EARLY-RECOGNITION-ACTION-AND-DIAGNOSTIC-CLOSURE-PROTOCOL-2026-11-07.md. Internal synthetic exercise, not a validated clinical pathway or authorized patient service. IRON-ENG-001 v1.0.0.

## Current guidance changes the implementation opportunity
ACOG released updated diagnosis guidance on February 20, 2026, Clinical Practice Guideline 11, March 2026. A presumptive clinical diagnosis and appropriate empiric management need not wait for surgical confirmation; evaluation/imaging can continue. NICE NG73 similarly supports appropriate parallel management, investigation and referral, and does not exclude endometriosis from a normal ultrasound. These are source-supported care principles, not clinical orders generated by DIS.

Keep two tracks parallel: (A) reliably implement established care under qualified local oversight, with patient preferences and safety; (B) investigate mechanisms and new diagnostics. Completion of all research is not a prerequisite for guideline-supported evaluation. Unknown mechanism is not permission to make an unsupported diagnosis, but it is not a blanket reason to withhold indicated care.

Delay figures generally concern symptom-onset or first-presentation intervals, not uniformly years of abnormal tests. WHO states there is no known prevention of disease onset. The proposed pilot targets avoidable delay, symptom burden and failed follow-through; it cannot yet claim prevention of endometriosis, infertility or irreversible progression. Clinical, imaging-supported and surgical/pathological evidence are distinct, not mandatory sequential stages. A negative scan does not exclude all disease, especially superficial disease; individualized laparoscopy remains an option.

## What has been built
research/three_study_cycle/early_action_exercise.py implements nine explicitly synthetic service scenarios, with ten tests. It checks persistent concern closed after normal imaging, surgery-only waiting, sent/accepted/attended referrals misclassified as resolution, overdue reassessment, missing ownership, barriers, patient choice and absence of human safety review. Concern status is an input premise, not an inferred diagnosis. Fixture dates are arbitrary teaching dates, not guideline response intervals.

The existing source cycle now runs the rehearsal after unit checks and retains early-action/early_action_rehearsal.json in its evidence manifest. The script never diagnoses, orders a test, prescribes, sends outreach, closes a patient case or claims health benefit. 'Documented workflow' means only that the synthetic record meets its limited documentation checks.

## Actionable operating design
Use the canonical protocol's first-signal -> human safety/differential -> concurrent appropriate care -> accepted handoff -> reviewed results -> shared next step -> benefit/harm review chain. Each real action needs a clinical owner, locally approved response interval, achievable service capacity, consent/choice, outcome measure and a stop/review rule. A diary is an optional aid, not a barrier to care. Monitoring is event- and protocol-driven, not indiscriminate repeat testing or a promise of real-time patient surveillance.

Preserve distinct symptom-onset, presentation, actionable-concern, agreed-plan and diagnostic-state clocks. Include every eligible symptomatic entrant, not just people eventually diagnosed surgically. Retain unresolved cases and uncertain dates. Assess symptoms/function, completed appropriate action, harms, unnecessary procedures, workload, cost and subgroup experience. An abnormal-test count, diagnostic label or referral dispatch is not a beneficiary outcome.

The canonical protocol's lived-experience, clinical-record access, data-use/ethics, comparative study design and authorized implementation requirements remain open. Authenticated reviewer acknowledgment, durable case storage/restore, recipient behavior and real health effects are not demonstrated by synthetic tests. No partner commitment, patient enrollment or external dissemination is asserted.

## Concurrent scientific repair
The age-partition defect is corrected: planned bands must be contiguous integer intervals from 20 through an explicitly chosen 49/50 boundary, whether or not participants occupy a gap or overlap. Existing falsification tests are retained and new tests challenge unobserved gaps/overlap, empty plans and malformed endpoints. The repair does not change the declared valid age-standardization designs.
