# ChainWeb research preservation and workstream boundaries — 2026-11-06

**ACTIVE MISSION:** Independently test whether an explicit ChainWeb evidence-to-decision-to-correction process improves the accuracy, contextual fit, fidelity and sustainment of implementation decisions **relative to fully competent CFIR 2022 + iterative PRISM/RE-AIM + FRAME-IS**, controlling for access to evidence, staff time, expertise and independent adjudication.

## Non-negotiable separation
- **Methods-validation intervention:** ChainWeb-supported decision-making. Primary outcomes: blinded expert gold-standard problem detection, unsupported material claims, correct action selection; additional: delivery-fidelity and sustainment planning, costs, equity and reproducibility.
- **Established healthcare EBIs** are comparison case material (e.g. mailed FIT outreach; navigation after abnormal screening). Their published efficacy does not establish ChainWeb efficacy.
- **Population health etiology** (six conditions: colorectal, lung, breast, endometriosis, PCOS, fibroids; 50 states, Mississippi/Texas/Colorado focus) is a separate valuable continuing research stream; do not change, delete or relabel its evidence as method validation.
- **Clinical/field implementation** remains contingent on real sites, clinical authority, valid denominator, ethics/data terms, trained teams, stakeholder involvement, outcome measurement, equity/harms and funding. No patient intervention or RPLICE private execution was performed by this methods preflight.

## Pinned follow-on — preserve, do not launch
After scientifically credible ChainWeb validation, **transfer verified knowledge into existing healthcare platforms** to reveal state-specific gaps and opportunities for equity improvements. Interoperate with HerHealth, Thriving Communities, Mission Transition and other owned or properly authorized systems, without rebuilding them or importing protected records without approval.

## Preserved existing research
1. The 50-state cancer and 3-state/6-condition analyses, original source manifest, missing state-specific women's-health data, competing causes and source-version audit; retain their limits.
2. Hinds/Harris and Colorado measurement discovery (late-stage incidence vs proportion; county is not city), exposure/occupational context, GIS and ECHO research under separate open PRs #57 and #58.
3. The three-setting contextual fidelity exercise: cross-trial outcome measures differ and are not a multicity randomized trial; retain its exercise and qualified conclusions.
4. Existing STELLAR 1, MAP–GAP, 19-stage implementation journey, human-selected SALP/Parallax/CFIR/RE-AIM interfaces and longitudinal decision guards. Do not duplicate.

## Fidelity and governance acceptance
- Claim provenance is original-source versioned, with correct population/time/numerator/denominator and contradiction record.
- Human method selection is distinct from authenticated specialist execution, independent scientific verification, participant consent and consequential clinical/organizational permission.
- Independent, predeclared evaluation must compare to a capable contemporary implementation-science alternative, not a weak straw-man baseline.
- Core intervention mechanisms and locally adaptable forms are separately tracked; reach, timing, dose, quality, patient experience, cost, harms and maintainability are measured.
- No superiority or patient outcome claim until justified independent ratings, trial/field observations and appropriate analysis.
- No GitHub Copilot or paid Replit agents without explicit user permission; GitHub main is source of truth and releases require exact-head tests.

**State:** Preservation register, not an active data transfer, clinical approval, outreach or completed external validation.
