# DIS Enterprise End States and Integrated Execution Program
**Version:** 2026-10-30 · **Authority:** owner review required before production release or consequential action
**Triage:** L4 GREEN (planned capabilities), with privacy/safety escalated under existing governance
**Source of truth:** `apps/dis-platform/lib/enterprise-mission.ts` (eight discipline contracts and six gate definitions) plus repository acceptance evidence. This document controls the program narrative, not permission to release.

## Command intent / end state

Create a domain-independent and evidence-grounded DIS intelligence and implementation system, capable of understanding complex problems, assembling the **minimum relevant and authorized specialist intelligence**, distinguishing evidence from assumption, collaboratively deciding, implementing under human authority, measuring actual outcomes and learning in a reproducible, reversible way.

**Never** interpret the 41 discovered repositories as 41 candidates that should be loaded. Many are client-specific or unrelated. Their public app front doors remain independent. The DIS user must see one connected journey rather than an app marketplace or speculative capability menu.

### Enterprise-level definition of success
A real representative user can (a) state a problem, (b) understand the proposed investigation and which disciplines matter, (c) accept/reject specialized evidence requests with clear consent, (d) inspect exact sources and dissent, (e) select a feasible strategy with resources/owners, (f) review and authorize a real deliverable/action, and (g) return after a defined observation period to compare baseline with outcomes and update the plan. No fake measurements; absent data remain unknown.

This is an intended outcome, **not certified today**.

Milestones represent **sequential evidence gates**, not calendar promises or checkboxes that an agent can self-certify. Each gate has a separate builder, independent reviewer, stakeholder acceptor, and evidence location.

| M1 Validate | Research question and evidence appraisal; competitor/alternative map; user journey; baseline | Research lead and affected stakeholders | Independent research/ethics reviewer | Falsifiable hypotheses, source claims, methods, privacy/IRB requirements and pilot measures checked | DESIGN / not accepted |
| M2 Integrate | Minimal relevant capability adapters, contracts, signed scopes, telemetry, negative tests | Engineering lead with domain stewards | Security and data steward | Both-way authorized contractual tests, 401/403/timeout/rollback, no tenant/client leakage | PARTIALLY CODED / live acceptance blocked |
| M3 Demonstrate | Complete user journey from question through evaluated decision artifact | UX and implementation lead | Pilot users + independent verifier | Mobile and desktop real-browser tasks, useful deliverable, readable uncertainty and clear next step | NOT VERIFIED for new fabric |
| M4 Measure | Pilot outcomes, implementation fidelity, usability, routing and cost | Evaluation lead | Mission owner / external evaluator | Before-after or appropriate design, meaningful denominators, errors and unintended effects logged | NOT RUN |

**Evidence minimum per gate:** artifact URL + immutable revision, environment, real execution timestamp, independent verification, failed checks, known exclusions, approving authority and rollback. No status becomes "complete" from a README, green build, automated acknowledgment, or marketing narrative alone.

## Proposed first bounded proof — Research-to-Implementation / Community Impact

**Candidate research question:** What prevents an evidence-informed community workforce intervention from reaching intended participants, being adopted consistently and producing measurable, durable economic mobility in a particular community?

**Purpose:** Demonstrate DIS independent methodology while selectively using a narrow amount of approved external intelligence. Do not pick a program, jurisdiction, participant population, or outcome value without the mission owner's evidence and approval.

1. **Sense and frame:** Define organization and jurisdiction; identify participating population, current operating model, decision rights, data permissions and baseline availability.
2. **Research and convergence:** Build falsifiable questions; critically appraise public original studies; compare designs, contradictory findings and external validity; describe methodological expertise needed. No university collaboration or endorsement is assumed.
3. **Implementation:** Review implementation determinants and possible study/evaluation designs; RPLICE contract requires independent production authorization before live calls.
4. **Context:** Access only public/approved aggregate community/civic and workforce measures at matched geography and reporting period. ThriveUp and Civic Signal stay in their own trust boundaries.
5. **Resources:** Funding/market research only if relevant and legal-entity-specific; do not borrow GrantPathPro client data.
6. **Challenge:** Convene distinct perspectives and critique causal overreach, unmeasured populations, selection bias, capability constraints, burden and potential inequity.
7. **Design:** Produce research logic model, measured implementation plan, resource needs, risks and human decision gates.
8. **Assess:** Conduct a bounded pilot when authorized; record real adoption, reach, feasibility, fidelity, outcomes, uncertainty, cost and unintended effects.
9. **Learn:** Report whether outcomes improved, which mechanisms were supported, what failed and what may generalize. Promote findings to other capabilities only with authorized review.

**First pilot acceptance:** 1 problem charter, 1 source register with claim-level checks, ≥2 contrasting methodological options, 1 stakeholder/authority map, a testable implementation and evaluation plan, and 1 reproducible, reviewed deliverable. These are **planned deliverables**, not results already achieved.

## University and research collaboration lane
This is a genuine capability domain, not a list of famous university logos or a partner claim.

- **Discover:** Search public scholarly, award and investigator metadata; retain DOI/URL, date, methods, geography, population, limitations, competing findings and source quality. Public metadata does not grant use of protected study datasets.
- **Design:** Define which disciplines must jointly frame the problem and how to integrate concepts and methods. Include social, biological, operational, policy, economic and lived-experience expertise as warranted.
- **Evaluate:** Compare possible designs and measures, including ethics, feasibility, participant burden, comparison groups, implementation outcomes and sustainability.
- **Engage:** Institutional relationships need named individuals, mutual fit and documented consent. A conversation is not a signed collaboration. IRB, intellectual property, data use, publication, funding and attribution require competent institutional decisions when applicable.
- **Transfer:** A completed RPLICE scientific method or DIS research brief may inform the other **only after** a valid contract, identity, source/data and consent check.

Methodological starting points:
- NSF Convergence Research: https://www.nsf.gov/funding/learn/research-types/learn-about-convergence-research
- NIH Dissemination/Implementation: https://research.nih.gov/research-at-nih/dissemination-implementation-research
- NIH NCATS CTSA partnerships: https://ncats.nih.gov/research/research-activities/ctsa
These are methodological background, **not validation of the particular study or of university partnership**.

## Risk / MAP-GAP register

- **Known:** Independent DIS research/consultation and read-only MCP foundations exist in source; RPLICE has a separate methodological tool catalog; ThriveUp/Civic exchange contracts exist; initial DIS private fabric is in PR #18.
- **Gap:** No authenticated production federation accepted; university agreements not established; owner-specific data authorization incomplete; no controlled user pilot of the new research mission.
- **Failure mechanisms:** Over-selection from client-specific repos; source metadata passed as verified evidence; unmatched geographic context; uncontrolled causal attribution; private keys or prompt transfer; invisible user actions; false "done" announcements.
- **Countermeasures:** Default-deny selection, non-advertising private capability fabric, claim-level evidence, explicit context matching, controlled consent, authorization-specific adapters, browser/tenant acceptance and human release.
- **No-go:** A real patient record, military information, campaign record, private client proposal or unconsented researcher information reaches a general DIS inference service.

**Release authority:** No merge, publication, client claim, human-subjects contact, external mail, partner key issuance, financial obligation or live data grant without the appropriate authorized human decision. Development code may be prepared and tested independently.
