# DIS Decision-to-Delivery Integrity Standard
Status: proposed addition, review-only. Canonical III doctrine and R1–R8 retrieval remain governing.

## Purpose
Preserve evidence, warranted uncertainty, council disagreements, consent and authorization from discovery through implementation and user-facing claims. The October 23, 2026 five-model nutrition review is the regression reference: the council authorized bounded G0–G2 research, while downstream builder language escalated some hypotheses to causal and regulatory certainties and described an immediate launch.

## Required records
Each consequential claim MUST have claimId, exact proposition, epistemicState (OBSERVED / INFERRED / MODELED / UNVALIDATED), source IDs and passage locators, known contradictions, affected stakeholders, freshness, reviewer and decision linkage.
Each decision MUST have decisionId, approvedScope, explicitlyDeniedScope, prerequisites, actor, approval status, dissent, expiration and rollback condition.
Each deliverable MUST trace its relevant claims and operations to approved decisions and evidence, or remain DRAFT/UNVERIFIED.

## Core invariants
D1: No observation without supporting source and applicable context.
D2: No causal inference from a null outcome without direct mechanism testing.
D3: No blanket "zero legal risk" or regulatory exemption without scoped legal analysis.
D4: No BUILD, RELEASE, SEND or DEPLOY authorization inferred from DISCOVERY approval.
D5: No consensus fabricated from omitted dissent; reconcile disagreements by evidence, not majority rhetoric.
D6: Separate simulated intellectual perspectives, actual model runs, and verified human expert participation.
D7: No simulated result, recommendation or internally generated score presented as independent verification.
D8: If source or authorization evidence is unavailable, mark UNKNOWN and fail closed for consequential actions.
D9: Every material finding creates an owned remediation entry with test, closure evidence, sustainment owner and expiry/review date.
D10: Scope effort proportionally to harm, uncertainty, reversibility and user-requested depth.

## Agent orchestration
1. Discover 5Ws/H, desired end-state, in-what-ways, unknown stakeholders and what-we-aren't-asking.
2. Route specialist council perspectives and SME disciplines. Each writes independent hypotheses and falsifiers; document tool/model identities.
3. Research via graph -> source passages -> abstain (R1-R8); record search strategy, original source, contradiction.
4. Run adversarial challenge: actively test alternative models, overlooked constraints, cost/benefit, time and risk.
5. Decide with explicit scope, vetoes and minority positions; never use a vote as proof.
6. Generate work breakdown, ways/ends/means, LOEs, owner, phases, milestones and quantitative acceptance measures.
7. Check downstream artifacts against decisions and source claims before any release. Reject unapproved scope expansion.
8. Pilot, compare to baseline, evaluate usability, safety, adoption, buyer economics and long-term sustainability.
9. Reopen evidence and decision gates whenever material evidence or operating conditions change.

## Report
Answer | Council/SME routing | Evidence and limitations | Alternatives | What was tested (raw artifacts) | Consensus and dissent | Decisions and authorizations | Implementation and outcomes | Open questions | Sustained follow-up.

## False-positive safeguards
Missing claim evidence does not prove the claim false. A failed integrity gate does not by itself prove an implementation is unsafe. Release gates must identify responsible human authority where required and permit documented exceptions only after approval and test evidence.
