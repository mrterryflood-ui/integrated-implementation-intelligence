# DIS RPLICE platform stakeholder assurance standard

Version1.1 | November8 2026 UTC | Owner Terry Flood | Applies to this repository when loaded

The platform and its outputs must be intelligible, source-grounded, reproducible, statistically defensible, clinically appropriate for their intended use, implementable in the actual setting and sustainable. This standard applies to earlier affected work as well as new work. It carries the owner's request for the rigor that software professionals, cancer/medical organizations, research and implementation scientists, patients, communities and operators would expect. It does not mean any organization or person has reviewed, endorsed, certified or used the platform.

## Required perspectives and evidence

| Perspective | Acceptance question | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Software engineering security and operations | Does the exact implementation preserve contracts, limits, identity, privacy, recovery and real workflow behavior? | Types, behavioral normal/negative/fault tests, source revision, distinct review, deployed-environment receipts where claimed |
| Cancer and medical evaluation | Does each instrument and action fit the condition, population, indication, benefit/harm balance and professional authority? | Current primary sources, independent qualified clinical review, separate screening/diagnostic pathways, comparator, capacity, harms and patient outcomes |
| Research and statistics | Do constructs, denominators, clocks, estimands and uncertainty support the conclusion? | Original inputs, definitions, executable reproduction, design/covariance/dependence checks, rival/disconfirmation tests and correction history |
| Implementation science | Can appropriate action occur under real local conditions and endure? | Ideal/operational/experienced/available comparison, reach/adoption/fidelity/adaptation, workforce/resources, process and patient outcomes, costs and sustainment |
| Patients families and communities | Is the evidence understandable, respectful, equitable and useful, including for people outside care? | Actual authorized participation and dissent, plain-language interpretation, access and burden, preferences, subgroup harms, privacy and accessibility |
| Operators institutions and maintainers | Who owns the handoffs and the day after success? | Accepted roles, capacity, follow-through, failure/recovery, successor, resources, monitoring and change/retirement triggers |
| GIS measurement and visual communication | Can a user follow the result without false precision or misleading visual realism? | Source-linked values, periods, units, intervals, true geometry/resolution/crosswalks, accessible interaction and actual layout/comprehension testing |
| Interoperability and external systems | Can another system preserve meaning and authority through API/MCP? | Versioned contracts, bounded actual-byte transport, input/output validation, denial paths, source/evidence state and permission preservation; exercised authenticated exchange before operational claims |

## Decision, action and beneficiary value

Every substantive output must identify its intended decision and beneficiary outcome. Connect the finding to what it changes, what remains unknown, competing and potentially interacting explanations, the next discriminating test, proposed responsible roles, resources and exit evidence. Distinguish an observed contrast from a process gap, a desired target and a causal intervention effect. Keep unknowns explicit. A correct number can answer the wrong question; validate fitness for the intended decision rather than declaring all alternative constructs wrong.

An exploratory comparative study can be justified under uncertainty. Preserve contrary evidence and revise or reject favored hypotheses; permission to study does not establish a mechanism or justify routine rollout. The full investigation record must retain the same decision brief and all added gaps shown to the user. A role becomes assigned only after acceptance. A referral, earlier diagnosis, stage shift or completed checklist alone does not establish patient benefit.

Separate intended users, outcome owners, potential buyers and budget authorities, sponsors/funders and beneficiaries. For each pilot state the job supported, competent existing workflow, proposed added value, and evidence that would justify adoption or payment. Buyer interest, willingness to pay, procurement, interoperability, incremental benefit and sustainable operating fit remain hypotheses until verified. Measure beneficiary outcomes, harms, equity, capacity, burden and costs alongside research and implementation performance; include eligible people who never enter the referral pathway. Charts and model output are supporting evidence, not the final mission outcome.

## Consistent longitudinal behavior

One measure ID cannot silently become a different construct, population or denominator. Preserve source and definition versions with every observation. A changed definition or source requires a comparability decision; do not render an arithmetic trend by keeping the same unit label. Keep original and latest context visible. Reject impossible dates, invalid percent/proportion values and non-finite derived arithmetic. Retain recalled onset, observed event, source publication, retrieval, receipt and decision times separately.

Missing information creates a qualified synthesis, sensitivity analysis, scenario or next discriminating test. It cannot create a fabricated observation, automatic causal conclusion or universal ban on research. Clinical effects, source authenticity, numerical reproduction, implementation reliability and measured benefit retain separate states. Actual reviewers and their disagreements must remain distinct from model-generated professional lenses.

## Engineering enforcement and existing-work remediation

Reuse the repository's existing IRON engineering and operating-contract checks. The full platform suite runs the relevant normal, negative, authority, measurement and transport regressions. Public MCP and protected research interfaces must enforce actual bytes before parsing; caller Content-Length alone is insufficient. Standardized-rate arithmetic must preserve event-count/person-time units. Longitudinal checkpoints use the existing strict calendar/scale rules. Trend summaries preserve definition/source drift rather than manufacturing improvement.

For every affected feature record the requirement, source, owner role, prior behavior, corrective change, regression, exact revision and independent disposition. The whole-platform gap matrix distinguishes verified fixes from production, human review and clinical-use obligations still unverified. Tests of documents or checkboxes alone do not enforce a scientific or clinical standard.

## Pilot and release sequence

Calibrate source and instrument → reproduce pilot → challenge mechanisms and uncertainty → qualify the setting and comparator → exercise complete workflow → assess benefit/harm/equity/cost → independently adjudicate → scale only within the validated scope. Longitudinal learning can revise the question, instrument or goal. Prospective models do not retroactively change what was knowable at an earlier decision.

No unsupported percent-complete, universal expertise, operational interoperability, live GIS, clinical accuracy or patient-benefit claim is permitted. Production schema/RLS/tenant denial, restart/restore, actual user comprehension, human scientific/clinical review and field effectiveness require their own observed evidence. Preserve absent evidence as an owned gate while authorized research continues.
