# DIS–RPLICE: coupled research and implementation protocol
**Version 0.2 · November 7, 2026 · INTERNAL RESEARCH DRAFT**

**Decision:** continue internal development and falsification. Do not claim validated novelty, superiority, equitable impact, institutional endorsement, clinical efficacy or research participation. Terry Flood reviews and reproduces the bounded results before any authorized Dartmouth discussion. This is an implementation-science methods proposal, not a cancer treatment protocol.

## 1. Contribution we are actually testing
The candidate contribution is an executable, source-aware connection between understanding a problem and changing the system that produces it. A number is carried with its construct, instrument, denominator, population, place, observation period, release version, uncertainty and source lineage. It is connected to competing explanations, a discriminating test, an accountable decision, an authorized action, actual delivery, outcome review and a trigger for re-examination. The goal is fewer consequential errors and more appropriate, sustainable action—not more diagrams, confident prose, linked records or features.

“Double helix” is a useful communication metaphor for two interdependent strands: research that revises understanding and implementation that tests and revises that understanding in context. It is **not a claim that the metaphor is new**. Meissner's 2018 LADDERS technical report explicitly used a DNA double helix for dynamic change in learning health systems. That directly relevant older source must remain in the prior-art review despite the main six-year literature window.[1]

The scientific hypothesis is incremental: under comparable information, expertise, time and resources, does the explicit DIS linkage reduce material measurement, causal, implementation and equity errors beyond competent use of established methods? A null or adverse result is a legitimate finding. An architectural synthesis can be useful without being the first synthesis or being empirically superior.

## 2. Prior-art map and limits of this search
This is a focused primary-source verification and design synthesis, not a registered systematic review or exhaustive novelty search. Publisher and author sources were inspected; no claim of completed Google Scholar verification, complete DOI deduplication, independent quality appraisal or patent novelty is made. A full novelty review must include adjacent sectors and negative/comparative evidence, not just health papers with familiar terminology.

| Established contribution | What it already provides | Candidate DIS addition to test, not presume |
|---|---|---|
| LADDERS, 2018 [1] | Dynamic change, data, evaluation, replication and sustainability, explicitly represented by a helix | Machine-checkable source/measure/decision/action relationships, rather than ownership of the metaphor |
| Equity-focused PRISM/RE-AIM, 2024 [2] | Context, co-creation, core functions and adaptable forms, equity across iterative planning and implementation | Whether enforcing traceable cross-system relationships reduces additional consequential errors |
| EHR-driven learning health systems and implementation science, 2024 [3] | Practical links between data, implementation and learning | Measure-level compatibility checks and outcome-to-explanation revision within this particular architecture |
| FRAME-IS, 2021 [4] | Structured documentation of implementation-strategy modifications | Link adaptation records to disputed mechanisms, source versions, observed equity and scheduled re-evaluation |
| Evidence Triangulator, 2025 [5] | LLM-supported extraction/synthesis across research designs | Preserve unknown versus conflicting evidence, audit dependence and outcome compatibility, and link justified decisions to delivery and follow-up |
| Modern difference-in-differences methods, 2022–2023 [6–8] | Assumptions, pretest limitations, sensitivity and modern design/inference | Expose missing prerequisites and descriptive anomalies without pretending the software establishes identification |

The cited frameworks are not obsolete or merely static. The comparator must be their competent, iterative use. Evidence Triangulator also shows that cross-design AI synthesis is already a research area; its existence is not a validation of DIS or permission to inherit its causal scoring assumptions.[5]

## 3. Coupled strands within the existing DIS lifecycle
This is a view of the existing STELLAR 1 and research-to-implementation workflow, not an additional platform or replacement framework. RPLICE retains specialist methods. Selection, permission to transfer data, actual execution and scientific acceptance remain separate events.

| Shared checkpoint | Research strand | Implementation strand | Equity question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orient | Define phenomenon, estimand where appropriate and competing objectives | Identify people, decision rights, intended benefit and existing services | Who defines the problem and whose priorities are missing? |
| Audit and map | Establish source definitions, instruments, periods and rival mechanisms | Compare designed, operational and experienced realities | Who is unobserved, misclassified, excluded or overburdened? |
| Diagnose gaps | Find the observation that would distinguish explanations | Determine whether the relevant handoff, capacity or resource actually exists | Does a proxy conceal differential access or measurement validity? |
| Align | Choose a justified design; preserve uncertainty and alternatives | Co-design feasible actions, core functions, adaptable forms and stop rules | Who gains, pays, waits, absorbs harm or loses access? |
| Act and track | Measure prespecified endpoints and test rival predictions | Rehearse, authorize, deliver and record fidelity and failures | Do eligible groups receive and complete the relevant pathway? |
| Evaluate and adapt | Reassess attribution, bias, heterogeneity and uncertainty | Verify outcomes, explain deviations and adapt without losing core function | Is aggregate improvement masking worsening outcomes or burden? |
| Sustain and re-map | Update theory, source versions and transportability assumptions | Assign successors, maintenance, funding and re-evaluation triggers | Does benefit endure for groups with the least capacity or voice? |

The strands connect through a versioned record, not through an automatically causal graph edge. An evidence record supports a claim only within its actual scope. A decision names an owner, authority, alternatives and unresolved dissent. An implementation record distinguishes proposed, authorized, delivered, reported complete, independently checked and sustained. A new source release, denominator change, adverse signal, contradicted hypothesis or contextual shift reopens the relevant decision.

## 4. Required measurement and action contracts
Every substantive statistic needs source identity and version; extract location; numerator and denominator; case/eligibility definition; construct and instrument; geography and population; observation versus receipt time; missingness and suppression; adjustment method; uncertainty; dependence on other sources; and a statement of legitimate and prohibited inference. Similar numbers do not establish equivalent phenomena. Similar labels do not establish equivalent denominators. Different studies may inform the same question without being numerically poolable.

Every proposed action needs a traceable problem, competing explanations, mechanism hypothesis, feasible discriminating test, resources, accountable role, authorization boundary, core function, permitted adaptations, fidelity denominator, beneficiary endpoint, balancing harms and next review. Software may prepare those fields; it cannot manufacture the institutional authority or evidence that they describe.

Equity is not a universal score. At minimum, record affected groups and reasons for selecting them; incomplete coverage and differential measurement validity; participation and decision rights; access, reach and completion; absolute and relative outcome differences with appropriate uncertainty; adverse burdens and resource allocation; and sustainment. A flagged subgroup decline is an investigation signal, not automatically a causal disparity or a statistically established harm. Suppression must remain suppression, not be reconstructed for a nicer dashboard.

## 5. Current engineering repair: beyond pretrend prose
The previous readiness screen could accept a written declaration that pretrends had been examined while numerically divergent trajectories were present. The new pure module computes, for matched pre-intervention years, treated and comparator slopes using actual elapsed years, the slope of their gap, gap range, endpoint change, maximum adjacent change and residual variation around the linear gap. Unmatched years are disclosed; missing values are not imputed. Work is bounded and numeric overflow is rejected.

This closes a specific software omission: the data now challenge the declaration visibly in the existing workbench. It does **not** close the causal identification problem. A zero estimated slope can conceal a nonlinear pattern. A constant observed preperiod gap cannot establish parallel untreated postperiod outcomes. Failure to reject a pretrend test can reflect low power; conditioning analysis on a successful pretest can introduce problems.[6] Sensitivity analysis and design-specific assumptions remain necessary.[7,8]

No p-value, universal slope threshold, equivalence margin, clinical threshold or automatic causal approval is introduced. The three-common-preperiod rule is explicitly a local descriptive-screen rule, not a theorem about how many periods establish validity. Observation units, serial correlation, staggered adoption, treatment heterogeneity, anticipation, competing changes and spillovers still require a qualified analysis plan.

## 6. Reproducible internal exercises
The accompanying Node 22 / Python package runs the actual pure TypeScript diagnostic and existing equity-assurance module. It requires no external account, API key, model call or dependency installation. Twenty-four focused diagnostic tests cover normal and hostile inputs. Four repository integration tests check the existing counterfactual wrapper. The browser test adds a divergent-trajectory challenge while preserving source, correction, equity and authentication-denial checks.

Nine published synthetic fixtures support owner reproduction: five pretrend cases and four equity cases. A separate Python implementation uses rational-arithmetic formulas to cross-check numerical outputs and source hashes. Because both implementations were prepared in the same development session, this is a cross-language computational check—not independent-investigator replication.

One intentionally difficult equity fixture changes group composition. Group A declines from 80/100 (80%) to 630/900 (70%). Group B declines from 180/900 (20%) to 10/100 (10%). The total nevertheless rises from 260/1000 (26%) to 640/1000 (64%). Both groups decline 10 percentage points while the aggregate rises 38 points. The existing module flags the adverse subgroup signal; it does not label the apparent aggregate gain an equitable or causal success. These are invented teaching numbers, not cancer observations.

The fixtures are transparent development examples, not a sealed held-out benchmark. They cannot be used to train the system and then presented as independent evidence of scientific performance. Terry's unchanged rerun tests computational reproducibility; external blinded investigators and new cases are needed to evaluate method effectiveness and generalizability.

## 7. Preserve and reconcile the evaluation protocols
The November 5 validation protocol specifies three equal-evidence conditions: A, competent current practice; B, competent established tools; C, integrated DIS **on paper**. Preserve this as the conceptual/process comparison. Its primary endpoint is the proportion of case decisions containing at least one independently adjudicated, prespecified critical error or omission. Do not replace that endpoint with a count of generated insights or code tests.

The subsequent established-methods-versus-ChainWeb casebook is a nested incremental comparison, not silent authorization to discard the three-arm design. Software benefit should be examined separately: matched digital established-tool and digital DIS conditions, equal information, preparation, analyst time, AI access and user-interface affordances where feasible. Otherwise additional time, interface quality or extra evidence can masquerade as a method effect.

Before confirmatory evaluation: independent content review, explicit amendment reconciliation, held-out case construction, preregistered assignment and analysis, blinded adjudication, disagreement retention, precision/false-positive measures and a justified sample-size plan. Measure interpretation accuracy, explanation quality, actionable decisions, comprehension, resource burden, equity-related omissions and follow-through. Test component ablations only as labeled exploratory analyses unless prospectively specified. The method must be allowed to fail, add burden or work only in a restricted context.

## 8. Colorectal research and unresolved gates
The official county tables inspected in this cycle report 2018–2022 late-stage colorectal rates: Hinds County 30.4 per 100,000 (95% CI 27.3–33.8), annual count 76 and late-stage share 63.3%; Harris County 23.3 (22.7–24.0), annual count 1,044 and share 63.4%.[9,10] These are historical registry summaries, not current patient outcomes or intervention effects. They are not city rates. Similar shares and different rates generate a research question, not a causal mechanism.

A new source-definition concern remains open: the repository calls the percentage denominator “staged incident cases,” while NCI interpretation material describes cases at all stages. Exact treatment of unknown stage must be resolved against the original release and methods before deriving quantities. No unknown-stage inclusion/exclusion is assumed here. Do not divide an age-adjusted late-stage rate by a crude case share to reconstruct all-stage incidence.

| Gate and owner role | Evidence needed for closure |
|---|---|
| Source/measure audit — registry epidemiology and librarian | Independent extraction; exact stage denominator, unknown-stage handling, period, registry methods, source version and uncertainty |
| Causal design — biostatistical lead | Comparable annual panels, real intervention timing, pretrend and sensitivity work, plausible comparators, competing changes and spillovers |
| Operational follow-through — implementation lead/data steward | Actual authenticated case-linked delivery, fidelity, user outcomes and reviewer records; existing private handoff is reused, not treated as field verification |
| Experienced reality — community methods lead | Actual authorized participant evidence, not Council personas or unverified stakeholder labels |
| Transportability — population-health lead | Hinds/Harris plus justified Colorado and other comparison geographies with aligned units and local context |
| Comparative validity — independent methods panel | Fair comparator, locked outcomes, held-out cases, real ratings, uncertainty and null/adverse results |
| Owner reproduction — Terry Flood | Clean rerun, hashes, expected outputs, failures preserved, interpretation agreement and explicit next-stage decision |

The first two research steps remain source reconciliation and a descriptive explanation map. Do not force a difference-in-differences analysis onto a question or dataset that cannot support it. No local partner duties, funding, data access, ethics approval or Dartmouth commitment is implied by these proposed roles.

## 9. Temporal stewardship and transfer
Examine the preceding decade of measurement definitions, policy changes and service conditions; current evidence and actual workflow; the day after apparent success, including demand, exclusion and support burden; and the next decade of maintenance, funding, governance and successor capability. Use condition-specific observation windows and plausible latency rather than a single universal dashboard refresh rule. Transfer questions and tested methods, not colorectal-specific evidence or causal assumptions, to other conditions.

**Release interpretation:** code tested is an engineering state; owner-reproduced is a computational state; independently replicated is a scientific state; sustained equitable benefit is an empirical implementation outcome. None substitutes for the next. External discussion follows Terry's review and explicit approval.
